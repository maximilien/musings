**Tweet 2 (Optional reply):**
The chunking problem is real.

You split documents to embed them—but chunking can split semantic meaning. A question might be answered by info spanning two chunks that never appear together.

No perfect strategy exists. Every choice has tradeoffs.

**Tweet 3 (Optional reply):**
When VDBs are wrong for the job:
• Precise search (exact SKUs, legal citations)
• Structured data (just use SQL)
• Small datasets (<10K chunks)
• Relationship-heavy queries (consider knowledge graphs)

**Tweet 4 (Optional reply):**
This is why I built weave-cli—to experiment across 10+ VDBs without lock-in.

Sometimes local, sometimes hosted. Different embeddings. Different chunking strategies.

You need flexibility to find what works.
