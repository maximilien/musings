## LinkedIn Post

BONUS new post: "AI Agents Meetup SF: One Year Retrospective"

In January 2025, Dave Nielsen, Alexy Khrabrov, and I asked: does the Bay Area need another AI meetup?

The answer was yes—because 2025 was the year of AI agents, and there was no dedicated forum.

One year later: 9 events, 4,000+ registrations, and an incredible community.

What we learned:
→ Themed meetups drive focused discussion
→ Practitioners want real stories, not demos
→ Mix big company + startup speakers for best results
→ Protocols and infrastructure are consistently hot topics

Speakers from IBM, Meta, Salesforce, Uber, Microsoft, Google, Sourcegraph, Temporal, and dozens of startups shared what they're building.

**Next up: Meetup #10 — "What's Next for MCP?"**

Full retrospective with photos from all 9 events: https://open.substack.com/pub/maximilien/p/ai-agents-meetup-sf-one-year-retrospective
